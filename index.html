What is simulated: fork, wait, waitpid, exit, atexit, sleep, getpid, getppid, exec* (shown as a note), printf family with real C formatting, scanf, malloc/free, arrays, pointers, structs and unions. Stdout buffering is modelled, so text printed without a newline before fork() is copied into the child, just as on a real machine. Infinite loops stop after a step limit. This is a teaching simulator; for exact results on edge cases, compile with gcc.
